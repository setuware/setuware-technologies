# Setuware Technologies — Copy deck & voice

## Voice
Corporate-professional, like an established digital agency (think Infosys/TCS digital arms, Toptal, Netguru, WillowTree). Confident, polished, benefit-led, concise.

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Body paragraphs ≤ 2 sentences on the homepage, ≤ 3 elsewhere.
- Lead with business value (credibility, growth, efficiency, scale, security), not with internal mechanics.
- Use formal, standard vocabulary: "end-to-end", "scalable", "secure", "transparent", "dedicated", "measurable", "on time and on budget" are fine.
- **Never use** chatty or clever-sounding lines, idioms or contrasts: "not likes", "actually", "wired in", "watched for you", "no surprises", "nothing to re-explain", "we pick the simplest tool", "plain English", "what broke in production", "boring", "your name on everything". No rhetorical questions in body copy (Figma headings excepted). No exclamation marks. No second-person micro-promises repeated in every section.
- **Banned:** seamless, cutting-edge, leverage, robust, innovative, empower, unlock, elevate, world-class, synergy, revolutionize, journey, delve, game-changer, holistic, best-in-class, one-stop, next-level, state-of-the-art, rockstar, ninja.
- Ownership ("you own the code and accounts") is stated **once** on the homepage (why-us) and once on pricing/about — not everywhere.
- **Prices appear only** in: the homepage "Flexible plans" section, /pricing, /estimate, and the pricing card on each service detail page. Not in the hero, not on service cards, not in FAQs except the cost question.
- Operational detail (deliverable lists, durations) belongs on service detail pages and /process, not on the homepage.
- No invented clients, awards, certifications, team size, years or metrics.
